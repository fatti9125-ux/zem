# Look C: Collage variants (short-form)

There are three sub-styles, each taken from one reference reel. Pick one per piece.

## C1. Editorial paper (U.S.-debt explainer, 47 s @30fps)
- **Background:** off-white textured paper #efebe3. Torn-paper edges for panels and transitions. Inverts to a black page for dramatic beats.
- **Type:**
  - black condensed caps for headlines
  - the key number in **red #d7261e**, huge
  - an italic serif for emotional words, with a **yellow highlighter block #ffe14d** swiped behind them ("*speeding up*", "*borrow*", "*biggest bill*")
  - tiny tracked caps for kicker lines ("BEFORE THIS VIDEO ENDS,")
- **Assets:** cut-out photos (the Capitol, stacks of cash, an hourglass, a farm vs. factories), people-icon grids (one person highlighted in a red circle), and torn black "redaction" strips used as bars.
- **Signature moves:**
  - a live counter pinned top-right for the whole video ("BORROWED SINCE YOU HIT PLAY $1,724,583"), ticking up constantly
  - line charts draw left to right with a leading dot and year tags
  - a ranked torn-bar chart where the subject bar turns red and gets a sticker ("BIGGEST BILL")
  - a red strike-through over a word, then a replacement word
- **Pacing:** only 3 hard cuts in 47 s. Everything builds in place on one page.

## C2. Surreal B&W collage (Hitchcock reel, 14 s @24fps)
- **Background:** light grid paper. The only accents are **red #d9261c and yellow #f5c400**.
- **Assets:** high-contrast B&W cut-outs: eyes, mouths, hands, heads sliced open with a red/yellow interior, film clapperboard, buildings on a red sun disc.
- **Signature moves:**
  - a burst of floating objects with radial "sticks"
  - a push-in through an eye as a transition
  - a figure flanked by mirrored mouths
  - a repeated-face echo trail (one face duplicated about 10× across the frame)
  - outlined rectangles zooming outward like a tunnel
  - a horizontal slice/glitch transition (1–4 frames)
  - a hypnotic radial spiral
- **Pacing:** a new composition every 1–1.5 s, with nothing static.

## C3. Retro mid-century (Fleming / penicillin reel, 15.5 s @30fps)
- **Palette:** flat fields of khaki #d8cf98, lime #d8dc6a, brick red #a51c12, teal circles #6fae98, and yellow sun #f2d23a. Halftone B&W photo cut-outs.
- **Type:** bold grotesk (Inter Black / Neue Haas). Multi-colour word stacks (`Scientist` black, `Alexander` red, `Fleming` orange). Mixed sizes on one line ("He noticed / Something / **Strange**").
- **Signature moves (frame-timed):**
  - Solid colour wipe (1 f) → empty field (~5 f) → subject **pops up from below with overshoot** (~8 f) while its label scales up from ~10% (~8 f).
  - A red panel wipes in from the left to split the frame, and text builds inside it.
  - Words slide in staggered, 4–6 f apart.
  - Callout tags: the leader line draws first (~6 f), then a yellow tag box slides out of its end ("Bacteria", "Mold").
  - Slow 3D tilt/rotation on a hero photo (a petri dish).
  - A car drives across a skyline collage, and the year types in ("1928").
- **Pacing:** something changes every ~0.5 s.

## Also seen: presenter + hero number (MrBeast-style reel, 9.5 s @15fps)
- Desaturated concrete wall with a B&W presenter. Colour is reserved for the hero object (a $100 bill) and **green digits #3ddc84**.
- The presenter rises in a black circle wipe. Then the digits scatter in and assemble into `$1,000,000,000` *behind* the presenter's head. Layer the text between the background and the person to give depth.
