# Look B: White-evidence documentary

Source: "Noah's Ark / Durupınar formation" documentary (32 clips, 640×360 @24fps). It uses a channel logo sting at about 1:10 (that channel's brand: never reuse it).

## Two alternating worlds
1. **Full-bleed footage.** Drone aerials, field crews, labs, archival photos, AI-generated historical recreations (photoreal, desaturated warm/teal grade). This is about 60% of the runtime. Each shot runs 2–5 s and *always* moves: a drone push, or a slow 3–8% Ken-Burns zoom or pan.
2. **The white stage.** An infographic space where evidence is laid out like objects on a light table:
   - background #eeeeee – #f4f4f4 with a **heavy grey vignette** (corners at about #9a9a9a) and subtle paper texture
   - every object is a clean cut-out with a soft drop shadow (offset 0, 8–14 px, blur 20–30, 35% black), so it looks like it's sitting on the surface

Cut or dissolve between the worlds. The white stage is where explanation happens. Footage is where atmosphere and proof happen.

## Palette
| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Stage | #f1f1f1, vignette to #8f8f8f |
| Labels on stage | charcoal #2b2b2b and navy #1f2f55, condensed caps |
| Labels on footage | white #ffffff with a soft dark shadow, large condensed caps |
| Highlight / outline / dimension | yellow #ffd400 – #f2c200 (outline strokes, dashed measuring lines, highlighter) |
| Map land highlight | lime/green #8fd14f with a lighter top-face, or yellow #f2d33a for a second country |
| Warnings / stamps / proofs | red #d4202a, pink paper #e88b95 |
| Data bars | green #1a8f2a (inside) vs grey #b5b5b5 (outside), red handwritten-style figures (38%, 3X) |

## Type
- Condensed bold caps (Bebas Neue / Oswald / Anton).
- Names on footage are huge (40–60% of frame width), sometimes split over two lines ("CAPTAIN İLHAN / DURUPINAR").
- Stage labels are small and placed directly under or beside the object ("TORAH", "NOAH'S ARK", "ARK SOIL / NEARBY SOIL").
- Big values sit above dimension lines ("515 FEET", with "300 CUBITS" under it in navy).

## Recurring set-pieces (with source clip)
1. **Portrait introduction** (01): an old B&W photo with a white border slides onto a dark wood desk and settles. The name label prints below it. Then a manila envelope stamped "SECRET MISSION / NATO CONFIDENTIAL / RESTRICTED" slides beside it, with photos fanning out of it.
2. **3D relief map** (01, 10, 11): a grey-white extruded world relief on dark blue sea.
   - The country of interest rises with a green or yellow extruded top and a big white name ("IRAN", "TURKEY", "ARMENIA").
   - The camera pushes in and tilts.
   - A white plane icon travels along a dotted path, and a yellow traced border draws around the country.
   - Pin dots with a city label ("SIRNAK").
3. **Photo card with outline tracing** (01–02, 11): an aerial photo appears as a *physical print* with a curled corner and shadow, sliding in from the right (about 14 frames, ease-out, slight rotation, then slow drift).
   - A yellow stroke traces the formation's outline.
   - Shape words appear as it traces: POINTED / CURVE / SMOOTH.
   - A yellow dashed dimension line draws across with "500 FT".
   - A question caption at the bottom: "COINCIDENCE OR SOMETHING ELSE?".
4. **Scripture/object row** (02–03, 07–08): cut-out Torah scroll, Bible, Quran or old manuscript slide in from the edges with labels (TORA / BIBLE).
   - Religion symbols (☪ ✝ ✡ ☸ ॐ Faravahar) build in a column with names.
   - The column then *scrolls vertically* as more items are added.
5. **Dashed excerpt boxes** (06): quoted text blocks in dashed-border boxes next to each tradition. A **yellow highlighter sweeps** left to right over key phrases (about 8–12 frames per line), and the camera pans down the column.
6. **Icon trio** (03): a planet in the corner, line icons with captions popping in one by one (RELIGIOUS SCHOLARS / GEOLOGISTS / ARCHAEOLOGIST).
7. **Measured object** (08–09): a cut-out ark on the stage.
   - A thin horizontal rule draws above it with "300 CUBITS", and a vertical rule with rotated "30 CUBITS".
   - A top view with "50 CUBITS" overlaid.
   - The value converts: "515 FEET" big above "300 CUBITS" small.
8. **Scale comparison** (09): a historic ship (WYOMING, 143 FT) cut-out sits beside the ark (515 FT), each with a rule and a label. Cut to a modern city or cruise ship for scale.
9. **Website / article on stage** (09, 13, 14, 25–26): a screenshot card floats on grey and the camera zooms to a heading with a yellow highlight. A paper or article on a **dark walnut desk** slides in, pages fan, and highlighted lines appear.
   - A rubber-stamp hand slams **CLOSED** in red (15).
10. **Evidence collage** (17–18): "PRIMARY PROOFS" in red block caps on torn pink paper. Photos, newspaper clippings and B&W images stack around it into a mosaic, each one sliding in with a slight rotation.
11. **Team graphic** (18): red circles pop on white, a cut-out headshot rises into each circle (one holds a flag), vertical stems drop, and a bracket joins them under "TEAM".
12. **Sample comparison** (19): two petri dishes (top-down cut-outs) "ARK SOIL" vs "NEARBY SOIL".
    - The dishes slide up, and a mini table with green/grey bars builds under them: "Potassium (K) Analysis", red "38%".
    - Then "Organic Matter Analysis" with a red "3X".
13. **Object turntable** (17, 20): a single rock or petrified-wood cut-out rotates slowly on the stage. Words appear either side ("AFTER YEARS" … "PETRIFIED WOOD").
14. **Equipment explainer** (22): a white 3D render of the device on the stage, with a spec line typing in ("ADVANCED HIGH-TECH MILITARY GRADE RADAR TECHNOLOGY"), then footage of it in use. A label card "GPR (Ground Penetrating Radar)" over the footage.
15. **Scientific data** (22–24): a 3D point-cloud of the site, GPR heat-map volumes in a wireframe box, and a red callout tab "Structure at depth".
16. **Expert card** (15, 24): the expert stands in the landscape and a big white name pops in beside them ("ANDREW JONES", "DR. BUCKNER" on a white-wall portrait).
17. **Vertical split** (24): 2–3 stacked footage panels on a blurred background.
18. **Icon circles beside object** (29–30): material icons (nails, planks, pitch) pop into red-ringed circles in a column next to the ark cut-out. Then a construction timelapse: skeleton → hull → complete ark.
19. **Cutaway illustrations** (27): a drill auger animating through soil strata, with a debris burst at each layer.

## Ident and ending
- A 2–3 s channel logo sting at about 1:10. Only use the user's own logo, if they have one.
- The ending is a calm drone pull-back over the site with a dissolve to black. No heavy graphics.

## Pacing notes
- 3–11 hard cuts per 30 s, and still frames are only 8–39%. Motion never stops.
- The stage graphics build one element per 0.6–1.2 s. Footage inserts between builds are 2–4 s.
- Chapter openers are often an atmospheric aerial plus narration rather than a title card. On-screen chapter titles are rare. Questions appear as bottom captions.
