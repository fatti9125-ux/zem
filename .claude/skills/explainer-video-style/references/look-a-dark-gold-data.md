# Look A: Dark-gold data explainer

Source: the GTA VI "$2 billion budget" video (35 clips, 640×360 @30fps, narration only, no music bed).

## Type
- Headline: Oswald Bold / Bebas Neue, all caps, tracking 0–20, gold with glow, top-centre at about 8% from the top.
- Hero number: same font, 2–3× headline size, centred, sometimes extruded as 3D gold.
- Micro-caption: 9–11 px equivalent (at 640 w), all caps, tracking +100, grey, under the number.
- Lower-third label: 2 px vertical bar, then small caps (`|DIRECT SALES`, `|GAME NOT RELEASED`). Stack downward one per beat.

## Persistent frame elements
- **Chapter progress line**: a 2 px line on the very top edge, gold or pink. It grows left to right across a chapter and resets at the next one. It's on almost every frame from minute 5 onward.
- **Chapter tag**: top-left, a small gold-outlined pill "PART 1", then the chapter title in white condensed caps ("PART 1 · BUILDING A LIVING WORLD", "PART … · THE VERDICT"). It stays on screen for the whole chapter. **Seen in the reference only. Don\'t use it in the user\'s videos: they asked for no corner labels.**
- Vignette 30–40%, light film grain, occasional floating gold dust particles.

## Recurring graphic set-pieces (with source clip)
1. **Rolling money counter** (01, 07): digits tick every frame, ease-out over 2–4 s, lock on the final value, then a caption fades in under it ("$1 BILLION / 3 DAYS").
2. **Day chips under counter**: `DAY 1 · DAY 2 · DAY 3` pills light up in sequence.
3. **Infinite product field** (06–07): 3D plane of box-art tiles, camera dollies back while the counter climbs (`12M → 56M → 135M → 230M+ COPIES SOLD`).
4. **Comparison bars** (02): blue bars for Hollywood films with values, then a gold bar for the subject that rises far taller. Labels under each bar.
5. **Sales curve vs flat line** (05): blue "normal game" spike-and-decay curve with labels RELEASE / SALES / FORGOTTEN, then a gold line draws flat and long, ending in a dot labelled "GTA V".
6. **Orbit diagram** (06): gold cube on an ellipse, rings orbit it with labels BUY / PLAY / SPEND / RETURN.
7. **Timeline rail** (01, 22): horizontal year rail, a dot travels along it, thumbnails pop above (CHILDHOOD / TEENAGE YEARS / ADULTHOOD). The big headline counts `1 YEAR → 12 YEARS` and console-gen chips are added.
8. **Team grid** (13–14): job tiles fill a grid one by one (PROGRAMMERS → DESIGNERS → 3D ARTISTS → ANIMATORS → SOUND → WRITERS → QA TESTERS → + SPECIALISTS).
9. **Low-poly 3D explainer** (09–12, 15–16): flat-shaded box cars and a city. Text callouts BRAKE / COLLISION, an overhead wireframe "simulation" with a legend, zoom out to "ONE STREET → ENTIRE WORLD". 11. **Skeleton pipeline** (14–15): mocap footage → dots → bone rig → shaded dummy. Headline `MOTION CAPTURE → SKELETON → ANIMATION`, the active word in cyan or gold. Action tags (RUN / DRIVE / TALK) appear at the bottom right.

## Title moments
- A 3D gold logotype or symbol ("VI") hovers over darkened footage with questions appearing beneath it, one at a time.
- Confetti-shard explosion (red, gold and white triangles) as the transition into a new act, with words swapping inside it (MONEY → TIME → EFFORT).
- A year in particles, which assembles into gold 3D digits ("2013").

## Sound (inferred from the edit)
Narration carries everything. Use whooshes on slides, a soft "tick" on counters and a thud on stamps. Any music should sit under the voice at about −20 LUFS.
